## Adoption boundary

W3C ORG supplies the separation between role taxonomies, posts, memberships,
people, and organizations and recommends SKOS for role taxonomies. PROV-O
supplies source-generation and invalidation provenance. OPM demonstrates that
occupational groups, series, positions, and job-family standards are distinct
classification objects. None of these sources supplies an employer-to-SOC or
employer-to-O*NET mapping; therefore only caller-supplied explicit bindings are
persisted.
