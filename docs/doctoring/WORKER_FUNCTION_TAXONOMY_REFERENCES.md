## Scope note

Worker-function ranks are ordinal positions copied from the published
DOT table; they are definitional scale positions and never fitted or
calibrated weights. Score interpretation and fairness remain governed by
AERA, APA, and NCME (2014), cited in the repository's measurement-boundary
ADRs ([ADR 0145](../adr/0145-psychometric-channel-weight-estimation.md)).
