# 2.12.17 — Leftover map complete-case coverage

## Added

- Period leftover maps now persist how many scored posts entered the
  complete-case Gabriel factorization (ADR 0183). Missing cells stay
  out of the map; they are never treated as zero.
- After seed, the leftover pair list is captioned
  “Leftover map used N of M scored posts (complete-case)” so a sparse
  post that was scored but excluded is visible as coverage, not as a
  fabricated pair.
