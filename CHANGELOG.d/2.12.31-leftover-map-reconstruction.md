## 2.12.31 — Leftover-map reconstruction

- Persist leftover-map reconstruction `R̂ = ξ_{1:2} · ζ_{1:2}` on leftover
  post–criterion pairs (ADR 0183). After `make seed`, closest and farthest
  leftover pairs sit above the member list with `R̂` next to leftover-map
  distance `d`; click opens that post. Omit the badge when reconstruction
  is missing. Never invent a leftover score. Signed reconstruction is
  stored, never clamped. Identity `U + R̂ = R` stays auditable.
