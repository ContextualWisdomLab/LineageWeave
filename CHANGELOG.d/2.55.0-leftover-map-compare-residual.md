- Caption leftover residual `R` on grouping comparison strip leftover-pair
  buttons through formatLeftoverMapResidual (ADR 0298). After `make seed`,
  closest and farthest leftover pairs sit above the member list with grouping
  comparison leftover-pair buttons that match leftover residual `R` on the
  pair row; click a post marker or a pair button opens that post. A missing
  or non-finite `R` omits that leftover-map comparison residual badge and
  keeps leftover-map unexplained leftover `U` when finite, leftover-map
  cross share `x` when finite, leftover-map unexplained leftover share `s`
  when finite, leftover-map explained leftover share `e` when finite,
  leftover-map reconstruction `R̂` when finite, leftover-map distance `d`,
  plus the strip coverage notes. Do not invent `R` from `Y` and `E` or from
  `U` and `R̂`. A finite negative leftover is shown, never clamped. The strip
  does not gain the leftover-map graphic. Never invent a leftover score.
  Never invent a theta. No new columns.
